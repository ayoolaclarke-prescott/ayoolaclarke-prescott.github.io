About Ayoola

A deliberately multidisciplinary profile.

I am a Behavioural Information Governance Strategist and Business Psychologist based in Trinidad and Tobago. My career has been built at the meeting point of two fields that are almost never combined, and that combination is the whole point.

My academic foundation was designed, not accumulated. An MSc in Business Psychology gives me the science of motivation, organisational behaviour, and managerial decision-making. A BSc in Library & Information Science gives me mastery of knowledge systems, records management, and information governance. A Legal Studies minor, completed entirely with A grades, gives me genuine fluency in the legal environment those systems operate within.

Together they produce a practitioner-level expertise rarely found in a single professional profile, and a vantage point from which the human and structural sides of data risk can finally be addressed as one.
