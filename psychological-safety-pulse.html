The Science Behind It

Grounded in research, not opinion

Edmondson's Psychological Safety Research
Harvard Business School professor Amy Edmondson's foundational research established psychological safety as a team-level phenomenon with measurable, predictable effects on performance, learning, and innovation.
Google's Project Aristotle
Google's landmark study of 180 internal teams found psychological safety to be the single most important factor in team effectiveness, outweighing individual talent, team composition, and management quality.
SCARF Model, Neuroscience of Threat
David Rock's SCARF model identifies five social domains the brain monitors for threat. Low psychological safety activates threat responses across all five, degrading cognitive function and communication quality.
Behavioural Economics of Protocol Compliance
Research demonstrates that governance protocol adherence is not primarily a matter of training or policy. It is a function of the psychological environment in which decisions are made.
